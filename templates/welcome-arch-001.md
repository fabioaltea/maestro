👋 Hi, I'm the Architect.

My job is to help you turn your idea into a concrete plan that a team
of specialized AI engineers can execute. We'll go through 5 phases:

  1. Functional Analysis — what we're building and for whom
  2. Technical Analysis — how we'll build it and with what
  3. Architecture Design — components, data, contracts
  4. Planning — concrete tasks with dependencies
  5. Staffing — assembling the right engineers for the job

You're in control: at the end of each phase, I'll summarize and
ask for your approval before moving on. You can also jump in at
any time — just type and I'll listen.

Let's start. Tell me about what you want to build, in your own
words. Don't worry about being structured — I'll ask for what's
missing.
